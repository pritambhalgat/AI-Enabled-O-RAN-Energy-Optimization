# Research Problem

## Problem statement

O-RAN introduces programmable control through RIC architecture, creating an opportunity for AI-driven adaptive resource management.

## Research question

Can a RIC-based AI controller improve the energy efficiency of Massive MIMO while preserving throughput and QoS?

## Hypothesis

An adaptive controller observing channel quality and traffic conditions can reduce unnecessary power consumption compared with fixed-power operation while maintaining acceptable QoS.

## Scientific comparison

1. Fixed-power baseline
2. Optimization-based control
3. Supervised ML controller
4. Reinforcement-learning controller
